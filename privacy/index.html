Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what RTYB — Run to Your Bets (“RTYB”, “we”) collects when you use runtoyourbets.com, and what we do with it. RTYB is run by Christopher David, [CITY, STATE].

What we collect

  • Usage data. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used: pages viewed, referring site, device and browser type, and country. It doesn’t use cookies or identify you; we only see anonymous totals.
  • Your email address, only if you sign up for the Running Tab, our newsletter. It’s stored with Beehiiv, our newsletter service, and used to send the emails you asked for. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Beehiiv’s signup form sets cookies to record which page you signed up from and that you’ve already signed up, so it doesn’t ask you again.
  • Settings on your device. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser (local storage). It isn’t sent to us.

We don’t ask for your name, address, payment details or betting history, and we don’t sell personal information.

Sportsbook links

Odds on RTYB link to sportsbooks through our odds partner, Gambly. Those links carry a partner code that tells the partner the visit came from RTYB, and RTYB may earn a commission when you sign up or bet there. Once you leave RTYB, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Who we share it with

Only the services named above, which process data for us, and anyone the law requires us to share it with.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Analytics are anonymous and aggregated, so there’s nothing in them tied to you.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, block or clear cookies in your browser, and ask us to see or delete the data we hold about you by writing to info@runtoyourbets.com.

Age

RTYB is for adults 21 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above.
