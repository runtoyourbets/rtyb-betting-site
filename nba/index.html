FAQ

Does overtime count in an NBA first half bet?

No. A first half bet settles at halftime. Overtime only counts toward full-game bets.

How are NBA first quarter totals set?

Books take the full game total, divide it by four and add a point and a half to two and a half. A 240 game usually gets a first quarter around 61.5 or 62.5.

What happens if the first quarter ends tied?

A 2-way first quarter moneyline pushes and you get your money back. The +0.5 or +1.5 side of the spread wins.
