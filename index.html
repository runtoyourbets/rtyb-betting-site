Run To Your Bets: first-segment betting

▾

Run To Your Bets is built around the start of the game. The first period in hockey. The first quarter and first half in basketball and football. The first inning and the first five in baseball. Early bets settle fast: you're a winner or a loser in 20 minutes to an hour, not three hours, and you're not sweating garbage time, an empty-net goal or a bullpen meltdown.

We do the homework. Odds from every sportsbook we track, with the best price outlined. Records, streaks, splits and trends for every team, graded every night at the closing line. The numbers are free. Run to yours with the numbers.

How first-segment betting works

You bet one piece of the game instead of the whole thing. A first quarter spread works like a game spread, just for 12 minutes. A first period total is goals in the first 20 minutes. Overtime never counts, and a tie on a 2-way moneyline gets your money back.

Example

BOS @ NY, 28-31 after the first quarter. NY -1.5 covers, the Over 57.5 cashes with 59 points, and the NY moneyline wins. That's three bets settled before the second quarter starts.

FAQ

What is first-segment betting?

Betting one early piece of a game: the first period, first quarter, first half, first inning or first five innings. It settles as soon as that piece ends. The full guide

Does overtime count?

Never. A first-segment bet only counts what happens in that segment.
