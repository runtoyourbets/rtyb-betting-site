WNBA first quarters and halves work like the NBA's: books start with the full game line and cut it up. The difference is the size of the numbers. WNBA quarters are 10 minutes, and full game totals usually run from about 155 to 175. A first quarter bet settles in about 25 to 30 minutes; a first half in about an hour.

## How books cut the game line

### Spreads

A small game favorite is usually laying half a point to a point in the first quarter, and about half the game number in the first half.

### Totals

Just do the math. A 160 game total means 40-point quarters. Get up into the 175 range and higher and you're looking at 45 to 47-point quarters. Like the NBA, books start from a straight quarter of the total and shade it from there, so check the number against the math before you bet it.

The first half is close to half the game total, shaded a point or two either way.

### Moneyline

First quarter moneylines on a small favorite land in the -120 to -130 range, same as the NBA.

## The bets

- **1Q / 1H spread:** the favorite has to win the quarter or half by more than the number. [Spread explained](/betting-guides/spread/)
- **1Q / 1H total:** the combined points, over or under. [Totals explained](/betting-guides/totals/)
- **1Q / 1H moneyline:** who's ahead at the end of the quarter or half. A tie usually pushes. [Moneyline explained](/betting-guides/moneyline/)

## How it settles

- A first quarter bet settles at the end of the 1st quarter; a first half bet at halftime.
- Overtime never counts toward a first half bet.
- A tie on a 2-way moneyline pushes; a whole-number line that lands right on the number pushes.

## What moves the first quarter and first half

- **Starters.** The first quarter is the starters' quarter. Check the injury report.
- **Depth.** The bench shows up in the second quarter. Thin teams fade in the first half.
- **Rest and travel.** WNBA schedules bunch games together. Back-to-backs show up early.
- **Pace.** It drives the total more than anything.

## Common mistakes

- Using NBA-sized numbers. WNBA quarters are shorter and totals are lower.
- Ignoring the math. If the game total is 168 and the first quarter is 44.5, know why before you bet it.

## FAQ

### How long is a WNBA quarter?
10 minutes, so a full game is 40 minutes of play.

### When does the 2027 WNBA season start?
Friday, May 7, 2027.

### Does overtime count in a first half bet?
No. First half bets settle at halftime.
