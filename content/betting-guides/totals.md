A total is a bet on the combined score. Over or under the number, that's it. On a first-segment total, only the first period, quarter, half, inning or five innings counts. Who wins doesn't matter.

## How books set first-segment totals

Every sport cuts the game total differently. Knowing how is half the bet.

- **NHL first period:** the base line is always 1.5. Many books offer alternate lines from 0.5 to 2.5 and higher, but the base line doesn't move, so the price does. A game with a 6.5 total gets a more expensive Over than a 5.5. Get two goals and you've got a winner. [NHL guide](/nhl/betting-guide/)
- **NBA:** the game total divided by four, plus 1.5 to 2.5 points. A 240 game gets a first quarter around 61.5 to 62.5 and a first half around 121 to 122. [NBA guide](/nba/betting-guide/)
- **WNBA:** do the math. A 160 game total means 40-point quarters; 175 and up means 45 to 47. [WNBA guide](/wnba/betting-guide/)
- **College basketball:** the first half is shaded under half the game total, by about 3.8 points on average this season. [College Basketball guide](/college-basketball/betting-guide/)
- **NFL:** the first quarter is 7.5 or 9.5, built so you need two scores. The first half is about half the game total, and the hook on the key numbers is everything. [NFL guide](/nfl/betting-guide/)
- **College football:** the first quarter usually sits at 9.5 or 10.5. Books cut the first half in half, but college first halves have scored more than half the points this season. [College Football guide](/college-football/betting-guide/)
- **MLB:** the first inning is NRFI or YRFI; the first five total covers five innings of the starters. A 7.5 or 8 game total usually gets a first five total of 3.5 to 4; a 9 or 10 gets 5, sometimes higher. [MLB guide](/mlb/betting-guide/)

## Pushes

A whole-number total can push: if a 24 first half total lands on exactly 24, you get your money back. Half-point totals can't push, and the NHL's 1.5 never will.

## FAQ

### Why is the NHL first period total always 1.5?
Most first periods have one or two goals, so 1.5 splits them best. Books move the price instead of the line.

### Is the first quarter total the game total divided by four?
In the NBA it's a point or two higher. In the NFL it isn't close: it's 7.5 or 9.5.

### Does overtime count toward a first half total?
No. First half totals settle at halftime.
