College football is bully ball. Sharks versus minnows, big spreads and heavy moneylines in the first quarter and the first half. Think in scores, like the NFL, but expect more of them: college teams get about five to seven possessions a half. A first quarter bet settles in about 50 minutes; a first half in about 90 minutes, sometimes more.

## How books cut the game line

### Spreads

First quarter spreads are mostly 0.5, 1.5 and 3.5. The first half spread is a little more than half the game number, around 57% of it. A 3.5 was the most common first half spread.

### First quarter total

The college first quarter usually sits at 9.5 or 10.5. Through Nov 19 this season, those two lines covered 59% of FBS games. Low-total games (under 50) see 7.5, 8.5 and 9.5. High-scoring games climb: a 60-point game total usually has a 13.5 first quarter, and 70-plus gets 15 to 16.5. A 17.5 is rare; it would mean getting over the key number of 17, and we didn't see one this season.

Once the game total is 50 or higher, you'll usually need two touchdowns to get a first quarter Over home. Weather and other factors can move it down.

### First half total

Here's the angle. Books cut the college first half right in half: a 60 total gets about a 30 first half. But through Nov 19, college first halves have scored 52.5% of the points. The favorites get it done early. That's bully ball.

A 34 to 40 first half needs about five or six scores. The most common first half totals this season: 23.5, 24.5 and 26.5.

### Moneyline

Big favorites carry heavy first quarter and first half moneylines. When it's sharks versus minnows, the price tells you.

## Three seasons in one

College football betting splits into thirds.

- **The first third is bully ball.** Mostly non-conference games, a lot of sharks versus minnows. Big spreads, heavy moneylines, and the favorites usually get it done early.
- **The second third, from about Week 5 or 6 to Week 9 or 10,** is high-stakes conference play. This is where the contenders separate from the pretenders.
- **The final third, the last four or five weeks,** is the jockeying: playoff spots, seeding, and teams fighting to become bowl eligible, which takes six wins.

Starting next season (2027), the College Football Playoff expands to 24 teams. Five spots are reserved: the four Power conference champions and the highest-ranked Group of 6 team. The other 19 are at-large, so more teams will be jockeying, and the committee decides. Style points and wire-to-wire wins matter. They always have.

## The bets

- **1Q / 1H spread:** the favorite has to win the quarter or half by more than the number. [Spread explained](/betting-guides/spread/)
- **1Q / 1H total:** the combined points, over or under. [Totals explained](/betting-guides/totals/)
- **1Q / 1H moneyline:** who's ahead at the end of the quarter or half. A tie usually pushes. [Moneyline explained](/betting-guides/moneyline/)

## How it settles

- First quarter bets settle at the end of the 1st quarter; first half bets at halftime.
- Overtime never counts toward a first half bet.
- A tie on a 2-way moneyline pushes. Most early lines carry a hook.

## What moves the first quarter and first half

- **Mismatches.** The bigger the talent gap, the faster it shows up.
- **Tempo.** Up-tempo offenses run more plays and push totals.
- **Weather.** Wind and rain pull first quarter totals under 10.
- **Key numbers.** 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21. Know where the hook sits.
- **Starters resting.** In blowouts, starters can come out early in the second half, which is one more reason first halves score more than their share.

## Common mistakes

- Treating college like the NFL. College first quarters start around 9.5, not 7.5, and first halves score more than half.
- Ignoring weather on low totals.
- Laying a huge first quarter number. Even bullies take a drive or two to get going.

## FAQ

### What's the most common college football first quarter total?
9.5, with 10.5 right behind it. Together they were the line in 59% of FBS games this season.

### Do college football first halves score more than half the points?
This season, yes: 52.5% of the points, even though books price the first half at about half the game total.

### Does overtime count in a first half bet?
No. First half bets settle at halftime.
