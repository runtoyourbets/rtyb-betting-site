First-segment betting is betting on just the start of the game. The first period in hockey. The first quarter or first half in basketball and football. The first inning or the first five innings in baseball. You pick a side or a total for that piece of the game, and once it's over, your bet is settled.

That's what Run To Your Bets is built around.

## Why bet the first segment

- **It settles fast.** A first period is about 35 to 40 minutes. A first inning is 15 to 20. You're not sweating the fourth quarter.
- **The starters play.** The first quarter is starters. The first inning and first five are the starting pitchers. You're betting the matchup you actually looked at, not the bench or the bullpen.
- **No garbage time.** No late fouling, no empty-net goals, no backdoor covers.
- **The lines are different.** Books cut the full game line to price the first segment, and they don't always cut it the way the games actually play out. That's where the numbers come in.

## The bets

- [Moneyline](/betting-guides/moneyline/): who's ahead after the segment.
- [Spread](/betting-guides/spread/): the favorite has to win the segment by more than the number.
- [Totals](/betting-guides/totals/): over or under the combined score.
- [3-Way Moneyline](/betting-guides/3-way-moneyline/): Team A, Team B or Tie (NHL first period).
- [Both Teams to Score](/betting-guides/btts/): does each team score in the first period (NHL).
- [NRFI and YRFI](/betting-guides/nrfi-yrfi/): a run in the first inning, or not (MLB).
- [First Five](/betting-guides/first-five/): the first five innings (MLB).

## The tie

The big difference from full game betting: first segments end tied a lot. Think soccer: Team A, Team B or the draw. Something has to win. NHL first periods have ended tied {{nhl.1p.tie:season}} of the time this season. On a 2-way moneyline a tie is a push and you get your money back. On a 3-way, the tie is its own bet. Know which one you're on.

## FAQ

### What is a first half bet?
A bet that only counts the first half of the game. It settles at halftime, and overtime never counts.

### Do first-segment bets count overtime?
No. Only the segment you bet counts.

### Which sports have first-segment bets?
All of them. On Run To Your Bets: NHL, NBA, WNBA, MLB, College Basketball, NFL and College Football.
