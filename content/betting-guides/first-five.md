A first five bet, F5 for short, covers only the first five innings of an MLB game. It's a bet on the starting pitchers before the bullpens show up. It settles in about an hour and a half, sometimes sooner if the pitchers are humming.

## The bets

- **F5 moneyline:** who's ahead after five innings. A tie usually pushes.
- **F5 run line:** baseball's spread for the first five, usually half a run. The favorite at -0.5 has to be ahead; the underdog at +0.5 wins if it's ahead or tied.
- **F5 total:** the combined runs after five innings, over or under. Books start with the full game total. A 7.5 or 8 game usually gets a first five total of 3.5 to 4. Go higher, a 9 or 10, and you're looking at 5, sometimes higher. It all depends on the starters and the lineups.

## It's about the starters, and the leash

The first five is built around the starters, not so much the bullpen. But the manager matters. How long is the leash on this pitcher? Will he get pulled early if he's in trouble? What's his pitch count? A 1, 2 or 3 guy in the rotation usually goes deeper.

Every lineup gets at least two at-bats against that starter, or whoever replaces him, in five innings. So do you trust those nine hitters more than the pitcher, or the pitcher more than the nine hitters? That's how you bet the first five.

## Ties happen

## What to check

- Both starters and how deep they usually go.
- Each lineup against the starter's throwing hand.
- The ballpark: dimensions, foul territory, altitude.
- Weather and day or night.

## How it settles

- Five full innings must be played, or most books void the bet. Check your book's rules.
- Only the first five innings count. What the bullpen does after that doesn't matter.

## FAQ

### What does F5 mean in betting?
First five innings. F5 bets only count the first five innings of an MLB game.

### What happens to an F5 bet if it's tied after five innings?
A 2-way F5 moneyline usually pushes. On the F5 run line, the +0.5 side wins.

### What if the game is rained out before five innings?
Most books void first five bets if five full innings aren't played.
