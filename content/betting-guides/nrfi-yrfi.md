NRFI means No Run First Inning. YRFI means Yes Run First Inning. Most bettors say them "NURF-ee" and "YUR-fee." NRFI wins if neither team scores in the 1st. YRFI wins if anybody does, top or bottom. The bet settles in about 15 to 20 minutes.

## It's starter vs starter

Each starting pitcher faces the first five hitters in the other lineup, maybe six. Get one or two guys on base and you've got a runner in scoring position. So the whole bet is: can those first five hitters get one run across against that starter?

Look at ERA, WHIP and strikeout rate. The bullpen has nothing to do with it. Lineups matter more than ever.

- Heavy offense against a bad pitcher leans YRFI.
- A good pitcher against a bad offense leans NRFI.

Nothing's guaranteed. A good pitcher can give up one run on a mistake pitch and then go scoreless the rest of the game.

## It's a coin flip, so the price is everything

The first inning stayed scoreless {{mlb.1i.nrfi:season}} of the time. When the bet is that close to 50/50, the price decides whether it's a good bet. -125 on a NRFI needs it to hit about 56% of the time to break even.

## The ballpark matters

Every ballpark is different. In 2026, the first inning stayed scoreless just 28% of the time at Coors Field in Colorado, and 63% in Baltimore. Texas (40%) and the Athletics (42%) were on the YRFI side; San Diego and Detroit (60%) on the NRFI side.

## What to check

- Both starters: ERA, WHIP, strikeouts, recent form.
- The top five hitters in each lineup, against the starter's throwing hand.
- The ballpark and the weather: wind blowing out, heat, humidity.
- Day or night, and who's resting.

## FAQ

### What does NRFI stand for?
No Run First Inning. It wins if neither team scores in the 1st.

### What does YRFI stand for?
Yes Run First Inning. It wins if either team scores in the 1st.

### How do you pronounce NRFI?
Most bettors say "NURF-ee." YRFI is "YUR-fee."

### Does the bullpen matter for NRFI?
No. It's the two starters against the top of each lineup.
