A spread evens out a mismatch. The favorite has to win by more than the number; the underdog can lose by less than the number, or win, and still cash. On a first quarter or first half spread, only that quarter or half counts.

## How it works

Say it's Team A -1.5 / Team B +1.5 in the first quarter. Team A needs to be ahead by 2 or more after the quarter. Team B +1.5 wins if it's tied, ahead, or behind by 1.

Most spreads are priced at -110 on both sides. Bet $110 to win $100. That extra $10 is the book's cut, the juice.

## How books cut it from the game line

- **NBA and WNBA:** a 3 or 4-point game favorite is usually laying half a point to a point in the first quarter, and about half the game number in the first half. [NBA guide](/nba/betting-guide/)
- **College basketball:** the first half spread is about half the game spread. [College Basketball guide](/college-basketball/betting-guide/)
- **NFL:** the first quarter spread is small; more than half of them this season were just half a point. The first half is closer to the full number, about 60% of it. [NFL guide](/nfl/betting-guide/)
- **College football:** first quarter spreads are mostly 0.5, 1.5 and 3.5; the first half is a little more than half the game number. [College Football guide](/college-football/betting-guide/)
- **MLB first five:** the run line, usually half a run. [First five explained](/betting-guides/first-five/)

## Pushes and the hook

A whole-number spread can push: -3 and the team wins by exactly 3, you get your money back. That's why most early spreads carry a hook, the half point. On 0.5, 1.5 or 3.5 there's no push. Somebody wins, somebody loses.

## FAQ

### What does -1.5 / +1.5 mean on a first quarter spread?
The -1.5 side has to be ahead by 2 or more after the first quarter. The +1.5 side wins if it's ahead, tied, or behind by 1.

### Is the first half spread half of the game spread?
In basketball, close to it. In the NFL it's usually a bit more, about 60% of the full game number.

### Can a first half spread push?
Only on a whole number, if the margin lands exactly on it. Spreads with a half point can't push.
