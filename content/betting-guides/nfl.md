Think of football betting as scores, not points. NFL teams only get about four to six possessions a half, so every first quarter and first half number comes down to how many touchdowns and field goals it takes to get there. A first quarter bet settles in about 45 minutes; a first half in about 90 minutes, sometimes more.

## How books cut the game line

### Spreads

The favorite lays a much smaller number in the first quarter: more than half of NFL first quarter spreads this season were just half a point. The first half spread is closer to the full game number, about 60% of it. A 3.5 was the most common first half spread.

### First quarter total: two scores

You're never going to see the first quarter total as the game total divided by four. The baseline is 7.5. Once the game total gets to 45 or higher, you'll see 9.5. Through Nov 19 this season, 7.5 was the line in 46% of games and 9.5 in 42%.

Both numbers are built so you need two scores. On 7.5, a touchdown plus a field goal or two touchdowns does it. On 9.5, three field goals (9) still loses; you need a touchdown in there. Get three scores of any kind and you can land anywhere from 9 to 21.

### First half total: key numbers

The first half total is basically the game total cut in half, and that's where the key numbers matter. Be very, very careful with the hook. A 41 total gets a 20.5 first half, so you need 21 to cash. A total near 50 gets 24.5 or 26.5, not 24 or 25.

If the first half total is in the 24 to 27 range, you need at least four scores. Three touchdowns is only 21. And the difference between a touchdown and a field goal is a 4-point swing every time.

The most common first half totals this season: 23.5, 20.5 and 22.5.

### Moneyline

Same direction as the spread: whoever's favored for the game is usually favored early, with a smaller price in the first quarter.

## The bets

- **1Q / 1H spread:** the favorite has to win the quarter or half by more than the number. [Spread explained](/betting-guides/spread/)
- **1Q / 1H total:** the combined points, over or under. [Totals explained](/betting-guides/totals/)
- **1Q / 1H moneyline:** who's ahead at the end of the quarter or half. A tie usually pushes. [Moneyline explained](/betting-guides/moneyline/)

## How it settles

- First quarter bets settle at the end of the 1st quarter; first half bets at halftime.
- Overtime never counts toward a first half bet.
- A tie on a 2-way moneyline pushes. Most early lines carry a hook, so pushes on spreads and totals are rare.

## First halves have scored less than half

Through Nov 19 this season, NFL first halves were priced at about 50% of the game total but scored only 48.6% of the points. Late-half drives and fourth quarters carry a lot of the scoring. Keep that in mind before you lay a big first half Over.

## What moves the first quarter and first half

- **Possessions.** Four to six a half. Count scores, not points.
- **Scripted openings.** Many offenses script their first drives. Some teams start fast every week; some never do.
- **Weather.** Wind and rain pull first quarter and first half totals down.
- **Game script.** A big favorite that scores early can sit on the ball. A team playing from behind throws more.
- **Key numbers.** 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24. Know where the hook sits.

## Common mistakes

- Dividing the game total by four for the first quarter. It's 7.5 or 9.5, not a straight quarter.
- Ignoring the hook. 20.5 and 21 are not the same bet.
- Betting a first half Over like it's half the game. NFL first halves have scored less than half.

## FAQ

### Why is the NFL first quarter total usually 7.5?
It takes two scores to get there: a touchdown and a field goal, or two touchdowns. Higher-scoring games get 9.5.

### Does overtime count in a first half bet?
No. First half bets settle at halftime.

### Is the first half spread half the game spread?
No. It's usually a little more than half, about 60% of the full game number this season.
