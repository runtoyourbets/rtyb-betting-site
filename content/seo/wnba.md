## FAQ

### How long is a WNBA quarter?

10 minutes, so a full WNBA game is 40 minutes of play. A first quarter bet settles in about 25 to 30 minutes.

### Does overtime count in a WNBA first half bet?

No. A first half bet settles at halftime. Overtime only counts toward full-game bets.

### What happens if the first quarter ends tied?

A 2-way moneyline pushes and you get your money back. The plus side of the spread wins.
