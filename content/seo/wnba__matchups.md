## Run To Your Bets WNBA First Quarter & First Half Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every WNBA game today with its first quarter and first half lines side by side: the spread, the total and the moneyline. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the quarter or half, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

LV @ NY, 20-20 after one. On the card, LV +1.5 is green (a tie covers the +1.5), the Under 41.5 is green (40 points), and both moneylines are yellow: a tie pushes. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Two strong starting fives push the first quarter total. A team resting a star changes everything.

## FAQ

### When do WNBA first quarter and first half lines come out?

Most books post first quarter and first half lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past WNBA games?

Use the date bar at the top of the page. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
