### How first-segment betting works

You bet one piece of the game instead of the whole thing. A first quarter spread works like a game spread, just for 12 minutes. A first period total is goals in the first 20 minutes. Overtime never counts, and a tie on a 2-way moneyline gets your money back.

### Example

BOS @ NY, 28-31 after the first quarter. NY -1.5 covers, the Over 57.5 cashes with 59 points, and the NY moneyline wins. That's three bets settled before the second quarter starts.

## FAQ

### What is first-segment betting?

Betting one early piece of a game: the first period, first quarter, first half, first inning or first five innings. It settles as soon as that piece ends. [The full guide](/betting-guides/first-segment-betting/)

### Does overtime count?

Never. A first-segment bet only counts what happens in that segment.
