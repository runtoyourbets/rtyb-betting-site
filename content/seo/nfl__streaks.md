## Run To Your Bets NFL Betting Streaks

Every NFL team streak in one place: 1st Quarter and 1st Half, then the Full Run (2nd Half and Full Game), the Full Run's with a gold dot. The markets are win or loss, the spread (cover or fail to cover) and the O/U in every segment. A streak counts every graded game in a row with the same result; a tie or push ends it. Home and road streaks count a team's home games only, or its road games only.

### How to read it

Pick the segment, the market and All, Home or Away. All shows each team's overall streaks, plus a home or road streak when it's longer than the overall one (tagged "at home" or "on the road").

- **This Week:** every game this week with a streak of 5 or more on the line, the longest first, up to 4 streaks per game. A home streak counts only when the team is home, a road streak only on the road. RIDE is the side that keeps the streak going and FADE the side that ends it, each at the best price; both open the matchup page with that market. STREAKS COLLIDE marks a game where two streaks point opposite ways in the same market.
- **Still Running:** every streak of 3 or more, longest first. The chip is the count, colored by length.
- **Run's Over:** streaks of 5 or more that ended last week, with the game that ended them.
- **Records:** the 5 longest streaks per market this season and last, with their dates. "Alive" marks one still running.

### Example

Say a team has failed to cover the 1st quarter spread 6 straight at home, and it is home this week. Its row shows a 6 chip, "1Q no cover" and "at home". RIDE is the other team's 1Q spread at the best price (the side that keeps the streak going); FADE is the team's own. A 2nd half streak shows "2H line posts at halftime" instead.

### What I look for

A streak is a fact about the past, not a reason the next game goes the same way. I use them to find games worth a closer look, then I check the price and the matchup before I bet either side.

## FAQ

### What counts as a streak?

The same result in one market, game after game, in the regular season and postseason: wins, covers, Overs, NRFI and so on. A tie or push ends it. Still Running shows streaks of 3 or more; This Week and Run's Over show 5 or more.

### What are home and road streaks?

A team's streak counted over its home games only, or its road games only. All shows one only when it's longer than the team's overall streak in the same market.

### Does a long streak mean it's due to end?

No. Every game is its own bet, and past results don't change the odds of the next one.
