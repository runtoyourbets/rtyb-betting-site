## Run To Your Bets NFL First Quarter & First Half Team Records

Run To Your Bets has a page for every NFL team with its first quarter and first half record {{season}}: win %, units, home and road, streaks, splits and a game-by-game log. Sort the list by any market, or tap a team for the full page.

### How to read the list

Pick a segment, a market and a side. Each team shows its record, its win % and its units betting it every game at the closing price.

### Example

Pick 1H, Spread, All: the list shows how every team has done covering the first half spread, and its units.

### What I look for

Look for teams that lead at halftime and blow it. They're a first half bet even when they're a bad full-game bet.

## FAQ

### Which NFL team is the best first quarter and first half bet?

Sort by units. The team at the top has made the most money betting it every game at the closing price. Win % alone doesn't tell you that, because favorites cost more.

### How often are NFL team records updated?

Every 10 minutes during the day, and every game is graded as soon as it goes final.
