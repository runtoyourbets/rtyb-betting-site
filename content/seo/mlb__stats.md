## Run To Your Bets MLB First Inning & First Five Stats

Run To Your Bets tracks every MLB first inning and first five result {{season}}: home and away, favorites and underdogs, Overs and Unders, with units at the closing price. As of {{asof}}, the first inning has stayed scoreless (NRFI) {{mlb.1i.nrfi:season}} {{season}}, the first five has gone Over {{mlb.f5.over:season}}, and the first five has ended tied {{mlb.f5.tie:season}}.

### Example

For example, a row that reads "NRFI 52% (1,262-1,167) +18.4u" means the first inning stayed scoreless 1,262 times and didn't 1,167 times, and betting NRFI every game at the closing price is up 18.4 units.

### What I look for

Look at the tie row on the first five. Ties are common, and they decide whether the moneyline or the +0.5 is the better bet.

## FAQ

### What is a unit?

Your standard bet. If your unit is $20, +5 units is $100. Units let you compare records no matter how much you bet. [More on units](/betting-guides/units/)

### Why does win % leave out ties and pushes?

A tie or push gives your money back, so it isn't a win or a loss. Counting it either way would make a record look better or worse than it was.
