## Run To Your Bets NBA First Quarter & First Half Team Records

Run To Your Bets has a page for every NBA team with its first quarter and first half record {{season}}: win %, units, home and road, streaks, splits and a game-by-game log. Sort the list by any market, or tap a team for the full page.

### How to read the list

Pick a segment, a market and a side. Each team shows its record, its win % and its units betting it every game at the closing price.

### Example

Pick 1Q, Spread, Away: the list shows how every team has done covering the first quarter spread on the road, and its units.

### What I look for

Find the teams that win the first quarter and lose the half. That's a team with no depth, and you can play both sides of it.

### Which NBA team is the best first quarter and first half bet?

### How often are NBA team records updated?

Every 10 minutes during the day, and every game is graded as soon as it goes final.
