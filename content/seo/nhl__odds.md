## Run To Your Bets NHL First Period Odds

Run To Your Bets pulls the NHL first period price from every sportsbook, exchange and prediction market we track and outlines the best one in green. Same bet, more money back. The odds update every 10 minutes, and the time of the last update is at the top of the page.

### How to read NHL first period odds

- **A minus price** is the favorite: -130 means you risk $130 to win $100.
- **A plus price** is the underdog: +113 means you risk $100 to win $113.
- **The line** (like Over 1.5) is the number the bet is graded against. The price is what it pays.
- **The best price** is outlined in green. When two books tie, both are outlined.

### Example

Say EDM is +113 on the first period moneyline at one book and +125 at another. On a $100 bet you win $113 at the first and $125 at the second. Same bet, same 20 minutes, $12 more. Do that every night and it adds up.

### The first period bets on this page

- **Moneyline (2-way):** who's ahead after 20 minutes. A tie pushes.
- **Total:** Over or Under 1.5 goals in the period. It can't push.
- **BTTS:** Yes if both teams score in the period, No if either gets shut out.
- **3-way moneyline:** home, away or tie. A tie wins only for the Tie.

Each one is explained in the [NHL First Period Betting Guide](/nhl/betting-guide/).

### What I look for

The 1.5 total never moves, so the books move the price. When the 1P Over is -160 one night and -125 the next, that's the game total talking. Shop it.

## FAQ

### Why are NHL first period odds different at every sportsbook?

Every book sets its own number and moves it with its own bets. That's why we show them side by side. The gap between the best and worst price is money you keep.

### What is the closing line?

The last price before the game starts. Every record on Run To Your Bets is graded at the closing line, so the units you see are what you'd have made betting at the close.
