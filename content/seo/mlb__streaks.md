## Run To Your Bets MLB Betting Streaks

Every MLB team streak in one place: 1st Inning and First 5, then the Full Run (Full Game). The markets are NRFI and YRFI in the 1st inning; moneyline, run line and O/U in the First 5 and the full game. A streak counts every graded game in a row with the same result; a tie or push ends it. Home and road streaks count a team's home games only, or its road games only.

### How to read it

Pick the segment and the market (the first of each is picked to start), then All, Home or Away. All shows each team's overall streaks, plus a home or road streak when it's longer than the overall one (tagged "at home" or "on the road").

- **Top Streak of the Day:** the longest streak on the line today, in its own card. It's never a 2nd half or later period streak.
- **In Play Today:** every streak of 5 or more on the line today, the longest first, with the opponent and start time under each one. A home streak counts only when the team is home, a road streak only on the road. The page is fixed for the day: streaks count the games before it.
- **Still Running:** every streak of 3 or more, longest first, with each team's next game under it (30 at a time, up to 100). Up Next lists the teams not playing today, in the order they play next. The chip is the count, colored by length.
- **Records:** the 5 longest streaks per market this season and last, with their dates. "Alive" marks one still running.

### Example

Say a team has gone NRFI 6 straight on the road, and it is on the road tonight. Its row shows a 6 chip, "NRFI" and "on the road". Under it: the opponent and the start time.

### What I look for

A streak is a fact about the past, not a reason the next game goes the same way. I use them to find games worth a closer look, then I check the price and the matchup before I bet either side.

## FAQ

### What counts as a streak?

The same result in one market, game after game, in the regular season and postseason: wins, covers, Overs, NRFI and so on. A tie or push ends it. Still Running shows streaks of 3 or more; In Play shows 5 or more.

### What are home and road streaks?

A team's streak counted over its home games only, or its road games only. All shows one only when it's longer than the team's overall streak in the same market.

### Does a long streak mean it's due to end?

No. Every game is its own bet, and past results don't change the odds of the next one.
