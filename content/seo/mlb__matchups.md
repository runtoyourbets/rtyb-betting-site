## Run To Your Bets MLB First Inning & First Five Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every MLB game today with its first inning and first five lines side by side: NRFI and YRFI, and the first five (F5) moneyline, run line and total. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the inning or the first five, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

NYY @ BOS, 0-0 after the first and 2-2 after five. On the card, NRFI is green, both F5 moneylines are yellow (a tie pushes), NYY +0.5 is green on the run line, and the Under 4.5 is green. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Starter against starter. Check both pitchers' first-inning numbers before you look at anything else.

## FAQ

### When do MLB first inning and first five lines come out?

Most books post first inning and first five lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past MLB games?

Use the date bar at the top of the page. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
