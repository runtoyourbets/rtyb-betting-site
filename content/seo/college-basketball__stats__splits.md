## FAQ

### What is a split in College Basketball betting?

A record cut by one situation, like home underdogs or teams on the second night of a back-to-back. It shows where the league's numbers come from.

### How many games does a split need before I trust it?

We hide anything under 5 games and only color a split after 10. More is better. A split that holds over a full season means more than a hot month.
