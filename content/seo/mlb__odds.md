## Run To Your Bets MLB First Inning & First Five Odds

Run To Your Bets pulls the MLB first inning and first five price from every sportsbook, exchange and prediction market we track and outlines the best one in green. Same bet, more money back. The odds update every 10 minutes, and the time of the last update is at the top of the page.

### How to read MLB first inning and first five odds

- **A minus price** is the favorite: -130 means you risk $130 to win $100.
- **A plus price** is the underdog: +113 means you risk $100 to win $113.
- **The line** (like Over 4.5 or -0.5) is the number the bet is graded against. The price is what it pays.
- **The best price** is outlined in green. When two books tie, both are outlined.

### Example

Say NRFI is -120 at one book and -105 at another. Risking $100, you win $83 at the first and $95 at the second. NRFI is close to a coin flip, so the price is most of the bet.

### The first inning and first five bets on this page

- **NRFI / YRFI:** does anyone score in the 1st inning?
- **F5 moneyline:** who's ahead after five innings. A tie usually pushes.
- **F5 run line:** usually -0.5 / +0.5. The +0.5 side wins a tie.
- **F5 total:** runs after five innings, Over or Under.

Each one is explained in the [MLB First Inning & First Five Betting Guide](/mlb/betting-guide/).

### What I look for

NRFI and YRFI live around -120 to +100, so the book with the better price is most of your edge.

## FAQ

### Why are MLB first inning and first five odds different at every sportsbook?

Every book sets its own number and moves it with its own bets. That's why we show them side by side. The gap between the best and worst price is money you keep.

### What is the closing line?

The last price before the game starts. Every record on Run To Your Bets is graded at the closing line, so the units you see are what you'd have made betting at the close.
