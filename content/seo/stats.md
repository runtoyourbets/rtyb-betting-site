## Run To Your Bets Stats

Run To Your Bets keeps the season record for every first-segment market in every sport: who wins the first period at home, how often first quarter favorites cover, how often the first inning stays scoreless. Every record shows its units at the closing price, so you know what it would have made you.

### How to read a row

The record, the win % (ties and pushes left out) and the units. Green is a profit, red a loss, yellow within half a unit either way.

## FAQ

### What is a unit?

Your standard bet. If your unit is $20, +5 units is $100. [More on units](/betting-guides/units/)

### Are college records Division I only?

Yes. College basketball counts Division I games and college football counts FBS games, so the records aren't padded by mismatches.
