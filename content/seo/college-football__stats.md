## Run To Your Bets College Football First Quarter & First Half Stats

Run To Your Bets tracks every College Football first quarter and first half result {{season}}: home and away, favorites and underdogs, Overs and Unders, with units at the closing price. ### Example

For example, a row that reads "Favorites 1H ATS 53% (410-362-14) +22.1u" means first half favorites have covered 410 times, failed 362 times and pushed 14, and betting every one at the closing price is up 22.1 units.

## FAQ

### What is a unit?

Your standard bet. If your unit is $20, +5 units is $100. Units let you compare records no matter how much you bet. [More on units](/betting-guides/units/)

### Why does win % leave out ties and pushes?

A tie or push gives your money back, so it isn't a win or a loss. Counting it either way would make a record look better or worse than it was.
