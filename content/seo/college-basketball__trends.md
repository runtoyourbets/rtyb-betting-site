## Run To Your Bets College Basketball First Half Betting Trends

Run To Your Bets grades every College Basketball first half trend every night. A trend is a situation and how it has done {{season}}: back road favorites, fade teams off a loss, the Over in divisional games. Every trend shows the league average next to it, so you can see if it's real or noise.

### The three sections

- **League Trends:** situations across the whole league, with the games today that fit them.
- **Streak Trends:** teams on a run of 3 or more in a row, at home, on the road or both.
- **Matchup Trends:** today's games, grouped by what kind of game it looks like: Fireworks, Lockdown, Mismatch, Heavyweights and Tug of war.

### Example

Take a league trend like "Back home underdogs". It shows its record, units and the league average beside it. With this many games, a trend can get to 100 games fast, and that's when I trust it.

### What I look for

Home court is real in college. I trust a home team's first half trend more than a road team's. And I don't chase a trend with a handful of games behind it. That's a coin flip with a story.

## FAQ

### How are College Basketball first half trends graded?

Every night, after the games go final, at the closing line. A trend's record and units only count games that had a line for that market.

### What makes a College Basketball first half trend strong?

How far it sits from the league average, and how many games are behind it. Strong means well past the base rate and making at least a tenth of a unit per bet. Moderate is a lean. Weak is shown muted, and it isn't a bet.
