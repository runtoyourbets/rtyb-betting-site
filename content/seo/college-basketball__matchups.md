## Run To Your Bets College Basketball First Half Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every College Basketball game today with its first half lines side by side: the spread, the total and the moneyline. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the half, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

UK @ DUKE, 34-36 at halftime. On the card, UK +2.5 is green (DUKE won the half by 2), the Over 68.5 is green (70 points), and the DUKE moneyline is green. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Tempo. A fast team against a slow team, check whose pace wins in the first half.

## FAQ

### When do College Basketball first half lines come out?

Most books post first half lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past College Basketball games?

Use the date bar at the top of the page. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
