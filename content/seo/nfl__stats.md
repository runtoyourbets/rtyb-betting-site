## FAQ

### What is a unit?

Your standard bet. If your unit is $20, +5 units is $100. Units let you compare records no matter how much you bet. [More on units](/betting-guides/units/)

### Why does win % leave out ties and pushes?

A tie or push gives your money back, so it isn't a win or a loss. Counting it either way would make a record look better or worse than it was.
