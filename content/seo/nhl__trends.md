## Run To Your Bets NHL First Period Betting Trends

Run To Your Bets grades every NHL first period trend every night. A trend is a situation and how it has done {{season}}: back road favorites, fade teams off a loss, the Over in divisional games. Every trend shows the league average next to it, so you can see if it's real or noise.

### The three sections

- **League Trends:** situations across the whole league, with the games today that fit them.
- **Streak Trends:** teams on a run of 3 or more in a row, at home, on the road or both.
- **Matchup Trends:** today's games, grouped by what kind of game it looks like: Fireworks, Lockdown, Mismatch, Heavyweights and Tug of war.

### Example

Take a league trend like "Back road favorites". It shows its record, its units and the league average beside it. If road favorites win the first period 58% of the time and every road team wins 49%, that's a spot. If it's 51%, it's noise.

### What I look for

I look for trends that line up with the starting goalies. A BTTS No trend with two backups in net isn't the same trend. And I don't chase a trend with a handful of games behind it. That's a coin flip with a story.

## FAQ

### How are NHL first period trends graded?

Every night, after the games go final, at the closing line. A trend's record and units only count games that had a line for that market.

### What makes a NHL first period trend strong?

How far it sits from the league average, and how many games are behind it. Strong means well past the base rate and making at least a tenth of a unit per bet. Moderate is a lean. Weak is shown muted, and it isn't a bet.
