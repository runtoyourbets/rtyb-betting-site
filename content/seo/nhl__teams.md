## Run To Your Bets NHL First Period Team Records

Run To Your Bets has a page for every NHL team with its first period record {{season}}: win %, units, home and road, streaks, splits and a game-by-game log. Sort the list by any market, or tap a team for the full page.

### How to read the list

Pick a period, a market and a side. Each team shows its record, its win % and its units betting it every game at the closing price.

### Example

Pick 1P, Moneyline, All: the list shows each team's first period wins, losses and ties, its win % and its units. A team at 80% (12-3-8) has won 12 periods, lost 3 and tied 8.

### What I look for

Sort by units, not win %. A team that wins the first period 60% of the time at -150 isn't making you money.

## FAQ

### Which NHL team is the best first period bet?

Sort by units. The team at the top has made the most money betting it every game at the closing price. Win % alone doesn't tell you that, because favorites cost more.

### How often are NHL team records updated?

Every 10 minutes during the day, and every game is graded as soon as it goes final.
