## Run To Your Bets NBA First Quarter & First Half Betting Trends

Run To Your Bets grades every NBA first quarter and first half trend every night. A trend is a situation and how it has done {{season}}: back road favorites, fade teams off a loss, the Over in divisional games. Every trend shows the league average next to it, so you can see if it's real or noise.

### The three sections

- **League Trends:** situations across the whole league, with the games today that fit them.
- **Streak Trends:** teams on a run of 3 or more in a row, at home, on the road or both.
- **Matchup Trends:** today's games, grouped by what kind of game it looks like: Fireworks, Lockdown, Mismatch, Heavyweights and Tug of war.

### Example

Take a league trend like "Back opponents of a back-to-back team". It shows its record, units and the league average beside it. A team on fresh legs against a team that played last night is exactly the kind of spot that shows up in the first quarter.

### What I look for

The first quarter is starters. I check the injury report before I trust any first quarter trend. And I don't chase a trend with a handful of games behind it. That's a coin flip with a story.

## FAQ

### How are NBA first quarter and first half trends graded?

Every night, after the games go final, at the closing line. A trend's record and units only count games that had a line for that market.

### What makes a NBA first quarter and first half trend strong?

How far it sits from the league average, and how many games are behind it. Strong means well past the base rate and making at least a tenth of a unit per bet. Moderate is a lean. Weak is shown muted, and it isn't a bet.
