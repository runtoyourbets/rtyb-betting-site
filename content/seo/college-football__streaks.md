## Run To Your Bets College Football Betting Streaks

Every College Football team streak in one place: 1st Quarter and 1st Half, then the Full Run (Full Game and 2nd Half). The markets are the spread (cover or fail to cover) and the O/U in every segment. A streak counts every graded game in a row with the same result; a tie or push ends it. Home and road streaks count a team's home games only, or its road games only.

### How to read it

Pick the segment and the market (the first of each is picked to start), then All, Home or Away. All shows each team's overall streaks, plus a home or road streak when it's longer than the overall one (tagged "at home" or "on the road").

- **Top Streak of the Week:** the longest streak on the line this week, in its own card. It's never a 2nd half or later period streak.
- **In Play This Week:** every streak of 5 or more on the line this week, in kickoff order, with the opponent and start time under each one. A home streak counts only when the team is home, a road streak only on the road. The page is fixed for the week: streaks count the games before it.
- **Still Running:** every streak of 3 or more, longest first, with each team's next game under it (30 at a time, up to 100). Up Next lists the teams not playing this week, in the order they play next. The chip is the count, colored by length.
- **Records:** the 5 longest streaks per market this season and last, with their dates. "Alive" marks one still running.

### Example

Say a team has failed to cover the 1st quarter spread 6 straight at home, and it is home this week. Its row shows a 6 chip, "1Q ATS L" and "at home". Under it: the opponent and the start time.

### What I look for

A streak is a fact about the past, not a reason the next game goes the same way. I use them to find games worth a closer look, then I check the price and the matchup before I bet either side.

## FAQ

### What counts as a streak?

The same result in one market, game after game, in the regular season and postseason: wins, covers, Overs, NRFI and so on. A tie or push ends it. Still Running shows streaks of 3 or more; In Play shows 5 or more.

### What are home and road streaks?

A team's streak counted over its home games only, or its road games only. All shows one only when it's longer than the team's overall streak in the same market.

### Does a long streak mean it's due to end?

No. Every game is its own bet, and past results don't change the odds of the next one.
