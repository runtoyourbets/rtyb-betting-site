## Run To Your Bets Trends

Run To Your Bets grades every first-segment trend every night at the closing line. A trend is a situation and how it has done this season, like road favorites in the first period or the Over in divisional games. Every one shows the league average beside it, so you can see if it's real or noise.

### Example

If home teams have won the first quarter 55% of the time and a trend says "home favorites of 3 or more win the first quarter 66%", that's 11 points better than the league. Over 50 games, that's a spot. Over 6 games, it's a story.

## FAQ

### How many games does a trend need?

League trends need at least 30 games before they show. Then they're rated Strong, Moderate or Weak by how far they sit from the league average and what they make per bet. More games, more trust.

### What are streak trends?

Teams on a run of 3 or more in a row in one market, like 5 straight first period Overs. They're on every sport's Trends page.
