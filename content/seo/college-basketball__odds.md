## Run To Your Bets College Basketball First Half Odds

Run To Your Bets pulls the College Basketball first half price from every sportsbook, exchange and prediction market we track and outlines the best one in green. Same bet, more money back. The odds update every 10 minutes, and the time of the last update is at the top of the page.

### How to read College Basketball first half odds

- **A minus price** is the favorite: -130 means you risk $130 to win $100.
- **A plus price** is the underdog: +113 means you risk $100 to win $113.
- **The line** (like -1.5 or Over 57.5) is the number the bet is graded against. The price is what it pays.
- **The best price** is outlined in green. When two books tie, both are outlined.

### Example

Say UK is +2.5 at -110 in the first half at one book and +3 at -110 at another. If DUKE wins the half by exactly 3, +2.5 loses and +3 pushes. With hundreds of teams on the board, the books don't all agree.

### The first half bets on this page

- **Spread:** the favorite has to win the half by more than the number.
- **Total:** the combined points in the half, Over or Under.
- **Moneyline:** who's ahead at the end of the half. A tie usually pushes.

Each one is explained in the [College Basketball First Half Betting Guide](/college-basketball/betting-guide/).

## FAQ

### Why are College Basketball first half odds different at every sportsbook?

Every book sets its own number and moves it with its own bets. That's why we show them side by side. The gap between the best and worst price is money you keep.

### What is the closing line?

The last price before the game starts. Every record on Run To Your Bets is graded at the closing line, so the units you see are what you'd have made betting at the close.
