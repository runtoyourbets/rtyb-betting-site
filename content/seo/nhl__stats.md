## Run To Your Bets NHL First Period Stats

Run To Your Bets tracks every NHL first period result {{season}}: home and away, favorites and underdogs, Overs and Unders, with units at the closing price. As of {{asof}}, the first period has gone Over 1.5 {{nhl.1p.over:season}} {{season}}, both teams have scored {{nhl.1p.btts_yes:season}}, and the period has ended tied {{nhl.1p.tie:season}}.

### Example

For example, a row that reads "Home ML 54% (190-162) +4.1u" means home teams have won the first period 190 times, lost it 162 times (ties left out), and betting every one of them at the closing price is up 4.1 units.

### What I look for

Start with Over 1.5 and BTTS Yes. Those are the two numbers that tell you what kind of season the first period is having.

## FAQ

### What is a unit?

Your standard bet. If your unit is $20, +5 units is $100. Units let you compare records no matter how much you bet. [More on units](/betting-guides/units/)

### Why does win % leave out ties and pushes?

A tie or push gives your money back, so it isn't a win or a loss. Counting it either way would make a record look better or worse than it was.
