## FAQ

### Does overtime count in a first period bet?

No. Only the first 20 minutes count. Once the first period ends, your bet is settled.

### What happens if the first period ends tied?

A 2-way moneyline pushes and you get your money back. On the 3-way moneyline, the Tie wins and both teams lose.

### Why is the first period total always 1.5?

Sportsbooks post the base first period total at 1.5 goals every night. Instead of moving the number, they move the price, so a higher-scoring game gets a more expensive Over.
