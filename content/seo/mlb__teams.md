## Run To Your Bets MLB First Inning & First Five Team Records

Run To Your Bets has a page for every MLB team with its first inning and first five record {{season}}: win %, units, home and road, streaks, splits and a game-by-game log. Sort the list by any market, or tap a team for the full page.

### How to read the list

Pick a period, a market and a side. Each team shows its record, its win % and its units betting it every game at the closing price.

### Example

Pick 1st Inning, NRFI, All: the list shows how often each team's games stayed scoreless in the 1st, and the units betting NRFI every time.

### What I look for

The top of the order decides the first inning. Teams that score early do it with the same three hitters every night.

## FAQ

### Which MLB team is the best first inning and first five bet?

Sort by units. The team at the top has made the most money betting it every game at the closing price. Win % alone doesn't tell you that, because favorites cost more.

### How often are MLB team records updated?

Every 10 minutes during the day, and every game is graded as soon as it goes final.
