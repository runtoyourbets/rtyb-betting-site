## FAQ

### Why is the first half total less than half the game total?

The second half has more free throws and fouling at the end, and the clock stops more. The first half usually plays faster and cleaner, so books set it a little under half.

### Does the clock stop on made baskets?

Not in the first half. The clock only stops on a made basket in the last minute of the second half and in overtime.

### Does overtime count in a first half bet?

No. A first half bet settles at halftime. Nothing in the second half or overtime counts.
