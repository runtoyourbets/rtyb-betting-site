## Run To Your Bets NHL First Period Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every NHL game today with its first period lines side by side: the moneyline, the 1.5 total, Both Teams to Score (BTTS) and the 3-way moneyline. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the period, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

EDM @ TB, tied 0-0 after one. On the card that's the 2-way moneyline in yellow (a push, both sides get their money back), the Under 1.5 and BTTS No in green, and the Tie in green on the 3-way. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Check both teams' first period records before you look at the price. Then check who's in net.

## FAQ

### When do NHL first period lines come out?

Most books post first period lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past NHL games?

Use the date bar at the top of the page. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
