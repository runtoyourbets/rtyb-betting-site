## Run To Your Bets NBA First Quarter & First Half Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every NBA game today with its first quarter and first half lines side by side: the spread, the total and the moneyline. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the quarter or half, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

BOS @ NY, 28-31 after one. On the card, NY -1.5 is green (NY won the quarter by 3), the Over 57.5 is green (59 points), and the NY moneyline is green. The full-game score sits underneath. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Fast team against fast team pushes the first quarter total. Slow against slow sits on it.

## FAQ

### When do NBA first quarter and first half lines come out?

Most books post first quarter and first half lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past NBA games?

Use the date bar at the top of the page. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
