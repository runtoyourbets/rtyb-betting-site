## Run To Your Bets WNBA First Quarter & First Half Betting Trends

Run To Your Bets grades every WNBA first quarter and first half trend every night. A trend is a situation and how it has done {{season}}: back road favorites, fade teams off a loss, the Over in divisional games. Every trend shows the league average next to it, so you can see if it's real or noise.

### The three sections

- **League Trends:** situations across the whole league, with the games today that fit them.
- **Streak Trends:** teams on a run of 3 or more in a row, at home, on the road or both.
- **Matchup Trends:** today's games, grouped by what kind of game it looks like: Fireworks, Lockdown, Mismatch, Heavyweights and Tug of war.

### Example

Take a league trend like "Back home underdogs". It shows its record, units and the league average beside it. A few points above average over a full season is a real edge in a league this small.

### What I look for

Starters carry the first quarter. Check who's in before you trust a trend. And I don't chase a trend with a handful of games behind it. That's a coin flip with a story.

## FAQ

### How are WNBA first quarter and first half trends graded?

Every night, after the games go final, at the closing line. A trend's record and units only count games that had a line for that market.

### What makes a WNBA first quarter and first half trend strong?

How far it sits from the league average, and how many games are behind it. Strong means well past the base rate and making at least a tenth of a unit per bet. Moderate is a lean. Weak is shown muted, and it isn't a bet.
