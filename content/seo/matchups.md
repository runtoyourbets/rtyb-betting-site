## Run To Your Bets Matchups

Run To Your Bets puts every game on today's board in one place, with its first-segment lines: the first period in the NHL, the first quarter and first half in basketball and football, NRFI and the first five in baseball. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see the score and how every bet settled.

### How to read a card

- The big number next to each team is the first-segment score. The full game is underneath.
- Green is the side that won, yellow a tie or push, and the losing side dims.
- Tap any game for its odds at every book, its trends and both teams' records.

### Example

EDM @ TB, 0-0 after one. The 2-way moneyline pushes (yellow), the Under 1.5 and BTTS No win (green), and the Tie wins on the 3-way. Four bets, settled in 20 minutes.

## FAQ

### Why don't I see a line for every game?

Some books post first-segment lines later than full-game lines. A game shows as soon as one book we track has a line for it.

### Can I see yesterday's results?

Yes. Use the date bar at the top. Every past day keeps its lines, scores and results.
