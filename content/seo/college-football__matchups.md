## Run To Your Bets College Football First Quarter & First Half Matchups

Run To Your Bets lists every College Football game this week with its first quarter and first half lines side by side: the spread, the total and the moneyline. Before the game you see the line and the price. After it, you see who won the quarter or half, the score, and how every line settled. Past days stay on the page, so you can go back and check any game.

### Example

ALA @ UGA, 7-3 after one. On the card, ALA +3.5 is green (ALA led), the Under 10.5 is green (10 points), and the ALA moneyline is green. Same game, every bet settled in one look.

### What I look for

Tempo against tempo. Two up-tempo offenses push the first quarter total; two ground games sit on it.

## FAQ

### When do College Football first quarter and first half lines come out?

Most books post first quarter and first half lines the day before or the morning of the game, after the full-game line is up. They show on this page as soon as one of the books we track has them.

### How do I see results from past College Football games?

Use the week bar at the top of the page. Every past week keeps its lines, scores and results.
