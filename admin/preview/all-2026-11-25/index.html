Alcorn State at Texas State: Back-to-Back Opponents Hit 90% in First Half

Texas State has won the first half 7 straight, and a 19-2 back-to-back trend fits Alcorn State at Texas State tonight.

By RTYB · Published 11:27 AM ET · NHL, NBA, NFL & NCAAB

Texas State is rolling into its 5:00 PM ET tip with Alcorn State. The Bobcats have won the first half 7 straight and covered the first-half spread 4 straight. Alcorn State has lost the first half 3 straight and failed to cover 3 straight. Fading teams off a blowout on the first-half moneyline is 60% (270-179), and that fits here too.

The rest of the card is deep. There are 14 NHL games, 9 NBA games, the Green Bay Packers at the Los Angeles Rams on Wednesday night and 58 college games. The Brooklyn Nets at the Milwaukee Bucks is the Matchup of the Day, and it pits two teams that keep losing first halves.
