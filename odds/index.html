Run To Your Bets Odds

Run To Your Bets pulls the first-segment price from every sportsbook, exchange and prediction market we track and outlines the best one in green. Same bet, more money back. Odds update every 10 minutes.

Why shopping matters

Example: NRFI at -120 at one book and -105 at another. Risking $100, you win $83 at the first and $95 at the second. Over a season of bets, the best price is the easiest money in sports betting.

How to read the odds

  • A minus price is the favorite: -130 means you risk $130 to win $100.
  • A plus price is the underdog: +113 means you risk $100 to win $113.
  • The line (-1.5, Over 57.5) is what the bet is graded against. The price is what it pays.

FAQ

What is the closing line?

The last price before the game starts. Every record on Run To Your Bets is graded at the closing line.

Which sportsbooks do you track?

The legal US books, plus exchanges and prediction markets where they offer first-segment markets. Every book we have a price from is in the table.
