College Basketball First Half Betting Preview · Wed, Nov 25

Alcorn State at Texas State Fits a 90% Back-to-Back Angle

Backing opponents of back-to-back teams is 19-2 in the first half, and Texas State has won the first half 7 straight.

By RTYB · Published 11:28 AM ET · 58 games

STAT: EDGE:

Alcorn State at Texas State carries three league trends tonight, more than any other game on the 58-game slate. The back-to-back angle on the first-half moneyline runs through it. So do the blowout fade and the back-to-back Over.

That same back-to-back angle also hits Lindenwood at Stony Brook, Grand Canyon at Colorado, Washington at VCU and Stanford at Saint Louis. Colorado and Stanford each play twice on this slate, starting with their 1:00 AM meeting.

League Trend

90%

Back opponents of a back-to-back team · 1H ML · 19-2

Opponents of back-to-back teams have won the first half 90% (19-2); tonight that's Lindenwood at Stony Brook, Alcorn State at Texas State and 3 more.

The Matchups

St. Bonaventure at Belmont · 10:00 AM ET

State Farm Field House, Kissimmee, FL · TV: ESPN2

Fading teams off a blowout has won the first-half moneyline 60% (270-179), and this game qualifies. St. Bonaventure's first halves have gone Under 4 straight. Belmont's have gone Under 5 straight. Both teams have played quiet first halves lately, and that cuts against any Over lean here.

Yale at Wright State · 12:30 PM ET · Matchup of the Day

State Farm Field House, Kissimmee, FL · TV: ESPN2

Wright State has won the first half 7 straight and covered the first-half spread 6 straight. Tonight Wright State lays -3.5 at the half against a Yale team that has struggled early. Wright State's halftime form is the hottest thing in this matchup.

Lindenwood at Stony Brook · 1:00 PM ET

Stony Brook Arena, Stony Brook, NY · TV: FloSports

The first-half Over in back-to-back games is 54% (28-24), and this game is on that list along with the back-to-back moneyline angle. Lindenwood has covered the first-half spread 3 straight, and Stony Brook has failed to cover 3 straight. Recent spread form leans toward the side getting points.

Akron at App State · 3:00 PM ET

State Farm Field House, Kissimmee, FL · TV: ESPNU

App State is the home underdog here, with Akron a favorite at -3.5 at the half after opening -2.5. The first-half Over in home underdog games is 53% (104-91). Akron's first halves have gone Under 4 straight. Two thin Over trends run into an Akron Under streak, so this total is a tug of war.

Alcorn State at Texas State · 5:00 PM ET

Thomas Assembly Center, Ruston, LA

Three league trends land on this game. Texas State has won the first half 7 straight, and its first halves have gone Over 4 straight. Alcorn State has lost the first half 3 straight. Texas State's first-half form is the loudest number on this card.

Grand Canyon at Colorado · 9:30 PM ET

Acrisure Arena, Palm Springs, CA · TV: CBSSN

This is Colorado's second game on the slate, after the 1:00 AM tip at Stanford. Colorado has failed to cover the first-half spread 5 straight and has lost the first half 4 straight. Grand Canyon's first halves have gone Under 3 straight. Colorado's slide at the half is the story here.

Washington at VCU · 10:00 PM ET

Lahaina Civic Center, Maui, HI · TV: ESPN2

Washington has won the first half 6 straight and gets +130 on the first-half moneyline. VCU's first halves have gone Over 3 straight. A hot road team at a plus price is the angle to watch.

Stanford at Saint Louis · 11:59 PM ET

Acrisure Arena, Palm Springs, CA · TV: CBSSN

Stanford also plays at 1:00 AM against Colorado, and this game carries both back-to-back trends. Stanford has lost the first half 3 straight and has failed to cover the first-half spread 3 straight. Stanford's recent form at the half has been as flat as anyone's on this slate.

Also with trends: Tulane at Davidson, Saint Peter's at Columbia, Arkansas-Pine Bluff at South Dakota, Ole Miss at Arizona and SE Louisiana at North Dakota.
