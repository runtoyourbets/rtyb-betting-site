College Basketball First Half Betting Preview · Fri, Nov 27

Fireworks expected between 'Canes and Tide

Omaha's first halves have gone Under 4 straight, and the late tip carries a first-half total of 68.5.

By RTYB · Published 8:18 AM ET · 49 games

STAT: EDGE:

# Jacksonville State at Omaha: Back-to-Back Games Have Gone Over 59% (44-30)

The last tip of the night is the one to watch. Jacksonville State visits Omaha at 10:30 PM ET in a back-to-back spot. Omaha is a home underdog. Both of those setups have leaned Over this season.

There are 49 games on the slate. Over in back-to-back first halves fits six of them, from St. John's at Iowa State early to the late one in Omaha.

League Trend

62%

Fade teams off a blowout · Game ML · 303-183

Fading teams off a blowout has hit 62% (303-183); tonight it fits St. John's at Iowa State, Vermont at Merrimack and 5 more.

The Matchups

St. John's at Iowa State · 1:00 PM ET

Michelob ULTRA Arena, Las Vegas, NV · TV: ESPN2

St. John's first halves have gone Over 3 straight. This is a back-to-back spot, and Over in back-to-back first halves has hit 58% (43-31). Iowa State is a +1.5 first-half home underdog, so the home-underdog Over trend fits too. Every trend on this game leans toward first-half scoring.

Maine at Le Moyne · 2:00 PM ET

Kirby Sports Center, Easton, PA · TV: ESPN+

The first-half total opened 65.5 and is up to 67.5. Le Moyne's first halves have gone Under 3 straight. The home-underdog first-half Over trend, 55% (120-97), fits here as well. The number has moved up even as Le Moyne's recent halves stayed low.

Arkansas-Pine Bluff at Missouri · 4:00 PM ET · Matchup of the Day

Mizzou Arena, Columbia, MO · TV: SECN+

Arkansas-Pine Bluff has lost the first half 7 straight. Missouri lays -24.5 in the first half. Missouri's first halves have gone Under 4 straight. It's a huge number, but Arkansas-Pine Bluff has trailed at the break every time lately.

Texas at Saint Mary's · 5:00 PM ET

Jenny Craig Pavilion, San Diego, CA · TV: Fox

Texas has won the first half 7 straight. Saint Mary's has won it 5 straight. Texas is a -4.5 first-half favorite on the road, and it's a back-to-back spot. The price favors Texas, but Saint Mary's brings its own run home.

West Virginia at North Carolina · 7:00 PM ET

Spectrum Center, Charlotte, NC · TV: ESPN

This game fits six league trends, tied with Maine at Le Moyne for the most on the card. West Virginia is a small first-half favorite at -1.5. North Carolina is a +124 home underdog. Fading teams off a blowout in the first half has hit 60% (286-192). There are no streaks here, just a long list of league trends.

Miami at Alabama · 8:30 PM ET

T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, NV · TV: ESPNU

Miami's first halves have gone Over 3 straight. The first-half total opened 79.5 and is now 80.5. Alabama is a +114 home underdog in the first half. It's the biggest first-half total on the slate, and it still ticked up.

Tennessee at Louisville · 9:00 PM ET

Michelob ULTRA Arena, Las Vegas, NV · TV: ESPN

Tennessee has won the first half 7 straight. Louisville has won it 6 straight. Tennessee is a small first-half favorite at -0.5, which brings in the small-favorite and small-underdog spread trends. Both runs can't survive the first 20 minutes.

Jacksonville State at Omaha · 10:30 PM ET

Resorts World Event Center, Las Vegas, NV · TV: FloSports

The first-half total sits at 68.5. Omaha's first halves have gone Under 4 straight. Omaha, a +3.5 first-half home underdog, fits both the back-to-back and home-underdog Over trends. The league trends say Over, and Omaha's recent first halves say otherwise.

Also with trends: Liberty at St. Thomas, Vermont at Merrimack, South Alabama at Fresno State, Southern at Vanderbilt, Texas State at Louisiana Tech, Charleston Southern at East Tennessee State and Pacific at UC Davis.
