College Basketball First Half Betting Preview · Fri, Nov 20

Virginia Tech Rides a 5-Game First-Half Win Streak Into Northwestern

Virginia Tech has owned the first 20 minutes lately, and Northwestern's first halves keep landing low.

By RTYB · Published 11:58 AM ET · 51 games

There are 51 games on the board, and the first one worth a look tips at 3:00 PM ET. Northwestern visits Virginia Tech, and the Hokies have been the better first-half team for a while now.

The bigger fade trends cover most of the evening. Fading teams off a loss on the first-half spread is 58% (157-116), and it fits 12 games tonight.

League Trend

60%

Fade teams off a blowout · 1H ML · 202-133

Fading teams off a blowout has won the first-half moneyline 60% (202-133), and it fits Northwestern at Virginia Tech, Manhattan at Miami and 9 more.

The Matchups

Northwestern at Virginia Tech · 3:00 PM ET

TD Arena, Charleston, SC · TV: ESPN2

Virginia Tech has won the first half 5 straight and lays -6.5 at the break. Northwestern's first halves have gone Under 3 straight, and the total has slipped since open. One side keeps winning the first half, and the other keeps playing it slow.

Manhattan at Miami · 5:00 PM ET · Matchup of the Day

Watsco Center, Coral Gables, FL · TV: ACC Network

Manhattan has lost the first half 5 straight. Miami has won it 4 straight and now sits at -20.5, up from the open. Manhattan has also failed to cover the first-half spread its last three. The market keeps asking more of Miami, and Manhattan's first halves have given it little reason not to.

Delaware at Cal State Northridge · 6:00 PM ET

Premier America Credit Union Arena, Northridge, CA · TV: ESPN+

Cal State Northridge opened -2.5 and has moved to -3.5. Delaware's first halves have gone Under 3 straight. Both fade trends fit this one too. The line is drifting toward the home side, and the Delaware total streak leans the same quiet way.

Bellarmine at Eastern Kentucky · 7:00 PM ET

Baptist Health Arena, Richmond, KY · TV: ESPN+

Eastern Kentucky has won the first half 5 straight and is -3.5 at home. Fading teams off a blowout on the first-half spread is 57% (121-93), and it fits here. A short home number sits on top of a team that keeps winning the opening 20 minutes.

Western Michigan at Mount St. Mary's · 7:00 PM ET

Knott Arena, Emmitsburg, MD · TV: ESPN+

Western Michigan has won the first half 4 straight and is the road favorite at -4.5. That makes Mount St. Mary's a home underdog, and the Over in home underdog games is 55% (50-41) in the first half. Western Michigan has also covered the first-half spread its last three. The road team's form and the home-dog total trend both put this one on the radar.

UT Arlington at Northwestern State · 7:30 PM ET

Prather Coliseum, Natchitoches, LA · TV: ESPN+

UT Arlington has covered the first-half spread 4 straight and is a small favorite at -2.5. Northwestern State's first halves have gone Over 3 straight, and the home-underdog Over trend fits again. The total has ticked up since open. This first half has been trending toward points from the home side's recent run.

Georgia at Georgia Tech · 8:00 PM ET

McCamish Pavilion, Atlanta, GA · TV: ESPNU

Georgia has won the first half 5 straight. Georgia, the favorite at -4.5, makes Georgia Tech a home underdog, and Georgia Tech has lost the first half 3 straight. The Yellow Jackets' first halves have also gone Over 3 straight. Georgia Tech's recent first halves have been short on stops and short on wins.

Mississippi Valley State at Arkansas · 8:00 PM ET

Bud Walton Arena, Fayetteville, AR · TV: SECN+

Arkansas opened bigger and now sits at -22.5. Mississippi Valley State's first halves have gone Over 3 straight, while Arkansas has gone Under 4 straight. Two opposite total streaks meet here, and only one of them can keep running.

Also with trends: Western Illinois at UAlbany, Mercyhurst at Canisius, Central Michigan at Pittsburgh, Loyola Maryland at Virginia, Niagara at Delaware State, Oakland at Purdue and Wagner at Maryland.
