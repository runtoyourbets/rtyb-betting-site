College Basketball First Half Betting Preview · Tue, Nov 24

North Dakota at Detroit Mercy: Fade Teams Off a Blowout Runs 60%

North Dakota has won the first half 4 straight, and Detroit Mercy has lost it 5 straight ahead of a 3:00 PM ET tip.

By RTYB · Published 11:21 AM ET · 77 games

STAT: EDGE:

There are 77 games on the slate, and North Dakota at Detroit Mercy is the best spot. Two league trends fit it: fading teams off a blowout on the first-half moneyline, and the Over in home underdog games at 54% (97-84). Detroit Mercy is the home dog, with North Dakota a -2.5 favorite in the first half.

The day starts at 10:00 AM ET with Wright State at St. Bonaventure, the Matchup of the Day. It runs late, ending with Stanford at Colorado at 11:59 PM ET.

League Trend

60%

Fade teams off a blowout · 1H ML · 251-165

Fading teams off a blowout has won the first-half moneyline 60% (251-165); it fits North Dakota at Detroit Mercy and Milwaukee at Florida tonight.

The Matchups

Wright State at St. Bonaventure · 10:00 AM ET · Matchup of the Day

State Farm Field House, Kissimmee, FL · TV: ESPN2

Wright State has won the first half 6 straight, and so has St. Bonaventure. Both teams have gone Under in the first half 3 straight. The back-to-back Over trend sits at 55% (21-17) and fits this game. One of these first-half win streaks has to end, and the total has already ticked down from its opener.

North Dakota at Detroit Mercy · 3:00 PM ET

Hard Rock Hotel Riviera Maya, Cancun, Mexico · TV: FloSports

North Dakota has won the first half 4 straight. It has also covered the first-half spread in all 4. Detroit Mercy has lost the first half 5 straight. The first-half line has moved toward North Dakota since the open. One side is rolling and the other keeps falling behind early, and the price has moved to match.

Miami at TCU · 3:00 PM ET

T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, NV · TV: ESPNU

Miami has won the first half 5 straight, and TCU has done the same. Miami is a -3.5 favorite in the first half, which makes TCU the home dog. That brings in the home underdog Over trend. Both teams have been winning first halves, and this total hasn't budged from its opener.

Long Beach State at Arkansas State · 3:30 PM ET

Ocean Bank Convocation Center, Miami, FL · TV: ESPN+

Long Beach State's first halves have gone Over 4 straight, and the back-to-back Over trend fits here too. Long Beach State has lost the first half 4 straight. Arkansas State has won it 3 straight. Arkansas State has the early form, and Long Beach State's halves keep landing high.

Milwaukee at Florida · 7:00 PM ET

Tower Hill Arena, Gainesville, FL · TV: SECN+

Florida has won the first half 7 straight. The first-half line has climbed from -18.5 to -21.5 in Florida's favor. The fade-off-a-blowout trend fits this game. That's a big number for one half, and the market keeps adding to it.

Nevada at Santa Clara · 7:00 PM ET

Acrisure Arena, Palm Springs, CA · TV: CBSSN

Two Over trends fit this game: back-to-back games and home underdog games. Nevada is a -1.5 favorite in the first half, so Santa Clara is the home dog. Santa Clara's first halves have gone Over 4 straight. Nevada has failed to cover the first-half spread 3 straight. Every Over angle here points the same way, and the total has already moved up from its opener.

VCU at Colorado State · 10:30 PM ET

Lahaina Civic Center, Maui, HI · TV: ESPNU

The fade-off-a-blowout trend and the back-to-back Over trend both fit this game. Colorado State has lost the first half 3 straight and failed to cover the first-half spread 3 straight. VCU has also failed to cover the first-half spread 3 straight. Neither side has been covering early lately, so the trends carry more of the story than recent form.

Stanford at Colorado · 11:59 PM ET

Acrisure Arena, Palm Springs, CA · TV: CBSSN

Stanford is a -2.5 favorite in the first half, which makes Colorado the home dog and brings in the home underdog Over trend. Colorado has failed to cover the first-half spread 5 straight. It has also lost the first half 4 straight. Both teams have struggled in first halves lately, so the total gets the attention in the late slot.

Also with trends: Rice at UIC, Tennessee at Maryland, Providence at Arizona, Mount St. Mary's at Morgan State, Central Michigan at Notre Dame and Florida A&M at South Alabama.
